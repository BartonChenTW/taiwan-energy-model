What this site is本網站是什麼

A model of Taiwan's electricity system built with PyPSA-Earth, an open-source energy system model. It takes Taiwan's power plants (from Taipower's own unit list), the transmission grid (from OpenStreetMap), hourly weather (ERA5) and demand, and finds the lowest-cost way to run the system for every 4-hour step of a year. The results, the inputs and every data source are published here. 以開源能源系統模型 PyPSA-Earth 建置的台灣電力系統模型。模型使用台灣的電廠（取自台電機組清單）、輸電網路（取自 OpenStreetMap）、逐時氣象（ERA5）與需求，計算一整年中每 4 小時最低成本的系統運轉方式。結果、輸入資料與所有資料來源都公開在本網站。

What it can and cannot tell you能與不能回答的問題

  • Good for: comparing options, e.g. whether more offshore wind or a nuclear restart cuts more CO₂, or where the grid limits supply.適合：比較方案，例如增加離岸風電或重啟核電哪個減碳較多，或電網在哪裡限制供電。
  • Not a forecast: 6 regions instead of the real grid, 4-hour time steps, a few weather years, and costs from international projections in euros.不是預測：以 6 個區域代表實際電網、每 4 小時一個時段、少數幾個氣象年，成本採國際預估值（歐元）。
  • Not official: this is independent research, not a product of Taipower or any government agency. Official figures are quoted with their sources.非官方：這是獨立研究，並非台電或任何政府機關的產品。引用的官方數據皆註明來源。
