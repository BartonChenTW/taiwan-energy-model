Why the first runs are not Taiwan's pathway為何最初的模擬不是台灣的路徑

The first runs (the early test and sensitivities A–D) allowed unlimited new nuclear and could not import hydrogen or other low-carbon fuels, so they built large nuclear fleets. That is not a realistic picture of Taiwan: its 2050 net-zero pathway has no new nuclear and relies on imported fuels. Those runs only tested the model. PyPSA-Earth's sector-coupled model also lacks options the official plan relies on; this fork adds them and runs the official pathway (below).最初的模擬（早期測試與敏感度 A–D）允許不設上限的新核電，且無法進口氫氣或其他低碳燃料，因此建置了大量核電。這並不符合台灣的實際情況：台灣 2050 淨零路徑沒有新核電，並仰賴進口燃料。這些模擬僅用於測試模型。PyPSA-Earth 部門耦合模型也缺少官方規劃所依賴的選項；本分支加入這些選項，並模擬官方路徑（見下方）。

Gaps found in PyPSA-Earth's sector-coupled model for TaiwanPyPSA-Earth 部門耦合模型用於台灣時發現的缺口

  1. No gas power plant with carbon capture (only district-heating CHP, which Taiwan hardly has).沒有燃氣發電搭配碳捕捉的電廠（僅有區域供熱汽電共生，而台灣幾乎沒有區域供熱）。
  2. No hydrogen or ammonia imports (export only); added in this fork for sensitivity C.沒有氫氣或氨的進口（僅有出口）；本分支為敏感度 C 新增此選項。
  3. Missing Taiwan data that silently distorts results: no demand growth rates (the default multiplies household demand about fivefold by 2050), zero cars (no EV chargers), and Taiwan listed as "Other Asia" in the UN energy data.缺少台灣資料，會默默扭曲結果：沒有需求成長率（預設值使 2050 年住宅用電增為約五倍）、汽車數為零（沒有電動車充電）、聯合國能源資料將台灣列為「Other Asia」。
  4. Bugs in the multi-year (myopic) chain, fixed in this fork: existing plants never retired, land-use limits counted existing capacity twice, converted biomass plants got unlimited free fuel, and clashing names dropped all links.多年期（短視）流程的錯誤，已在本分支修正：既有電廠從不除役、土地利用上限重複扣除既有容量、轉換後的生質能電廠獲得無限免費燃料、名稱衝突導致所有連結遺失。
  5. European defaults: 200 Mt/yr CO₂ storage, no floating offshore wind, zero geothermal investment cost.歐洲預設值：每年 200 Mt CO₂ 封存、沒有浮動式離岸風電、地熱投資成本為零。

First step: Taiwan's official pathway in this model第一步：在本模型中建立台灣官方路徑

The fork now offers what the official plan relies on: gas combined cycle with carbon capture (NETL cost and efficiency), hydrogen and ammonia turbines, and imports of hydrogen, ammonia, synthetic oil and synthetic methane, priced after Hampp et al. (2023). Offshore wind (40–55 GW), solar (40–80 GW) and geothermal (3–6.2 GW) are held to the action plans' 2050 ranges, and new nuclear is capped at zero unless stated. Pick the runs in the scenario menu below.本分支已加入官方規劃所依賴的選項：燃氣複循環搭配碳捕捉（採 NETL 成本與效率）、氫能與氨渦輪機，以及氫氣、氨、合成燃油與合成甲烷的進口（價格依 Hampp 等人 2023 年研究）。離岸風電（40–55 GW）、太陽光電（40–80 GW）與地熱（3–6.2 GW）限於行動計畫的 2050 年範圍；除另有說明外，新核電上限為零。可於下方情境選單中選取各模擬。

  • With the official options and imports, net zero in 2050 is reached without new nuclear and without unmet demand.採用官方選項與進口後，2050 年不新建核電也能達成淨零，且沒有未供應的需求。
  • When every carrier can be imported, the model buys about 310 TWh a year: synthetic methane 120, ammonia 93 (all of it burned in ammonia turbines for power), synthetic oil 92 and almost no hydrogen. The earlier result of 300–440 TWh of imported hydrogen came from hydrogen being the only carrier the model could import; the amount of imported energy is what holds.當所有能源載體都可進口時，模型每年進口約 310 TWh：合成甲烷 120、氨 93（全部用於氨渦輪機發電）、合成燃油 92，氫氣幾乎為零。先前進口 300–440 TWh 氫氣的結果，是因為氫氣是模型唯一可進口的載體；真正穩健的是進口能源的總量。
  • Nuclear is not a question of cost here: even when allowed, the model builds none at these import prices or 25% lower. Only at 1.5 times the prices does it build 17 GW, which saves €2.5 bn a year (4%) and cuts imports from 278 to 171 TWh. The real trade-off is import dependence, which matters for a blockade.在此核電不是成本問題：即使允許興建，在這些進口價格或低 25% 時模型都不會興建。只有在價格為 1.5 倍時才興建 17 GW，每年節省 25 億歐元（4%），並將進口從 278 TWh 降至 171 TWh。真正的取捨在於進口依賴，這對封鎖情境至關重要。
  • Gas with carbon capture is built only when the official mix forces 20% thermal power; it would need part of the 40 Mt a year of CO₂ storage.只有在官方電力結構要求 20% 火力發電時才會興建燃氣碳捕捉電廠；它需要占用每年 40 Mt CO₂ 封存量的一部分。
  • Still open: exposure of an import-based system to a blockade, 4-hour time steps, the 2050 CO₂ storage volume, and import prices estimated for delivery to Germany rather than Taiwan.尚待處理：以進口為主的系統面對封鎖的風險、每 4 小時一個時段的模擬、2050 年 CO₂ 封存量，以及進口價格係以運至德國而非台灣估算。

    Problems found發現的問題

      Resolved: an earlier version of this page reported 31.9 GW of coal and 44.9 GW of gas. Those were fuel-input ratings: the sector model turns each plant into a link rated on its fuel side (electric capacity ÷ efficiency). The electric capacities are the official fleet's.已釐清：本頁先前列出燃煤 31.9 GW、燃氣 44.9 GW，那是燃料輸入端的額定值：部門模型把每座電廠轉為以燃料端計量的連結（電力容量 ÷ 效率）。電力容量與官方機組一致。

      What was changed to make it run為了執行所做的修改

      1. The UN energy statistics report Taiwan as "Other Asia", which PyPSA-Earth dropped, so every Taiwan demand was zero. Mapped to Taiwan (checked: 2019 electricity production 274.2 TWh, nuclear 3,872 MW, pumped hydro 2,602 MW).聯合國能源統計將台灣列為「Other Asia」，PyPSA-Earth 因此將其排除，所有台灣需求皆為零。已對應為台灣（核對：2019 年發電量 274.2 TWh、核能 3,872 MW、抽蓄水力 2,602 MW）。
      2. The UN urban-population file moved to a new ID; the script now looks it up. Its name "China, Taiwan Province of" matched two countries and was dropped; mapped to Taiwan.聯合國都市人口檔案的編號已變更，程式改為自動查詢；其名稱「China, Taiwan Province of」同時對應兩個國家而遭排除，已對應為台灣。
      3. The World Port Index could not be downloaded by Python (certificate error); a local copy is used instead.世界港口索引因憑證錯誤無法由 Python 下載，改用本機副本。

      Next steps before publishing發布前的下一步

      1. Decide whether rooftop solar and biomass CHP may be built, or fix them at today's capacity for the 2025 reference.決定是否允許新建屋頂型太陽光電與生質能汽電共生，或在 2025 年基準情境中固定為現有容量。
      2. Check each demand against the Energy Administration's energy balance and align the scope (national vs Taipower system).逐項與能源署能源平衡表核對需求，並統一範圍（全國或台電系統）。
      3. Decide which technologies are fixed and which the model may build; set a CO₂ path.決定哪些技術固定、哪些可由模型新建；設定 CO₂ 路徑。
      4. Use finer time steps (e.g. 3–4 hours) once the inputs are right.輸入正確後，改用較細的時間步長（例如 3–4 小時）。
