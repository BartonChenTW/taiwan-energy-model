Problems found發現的問題

    Resolved: an earlier version of this page reported 31.9 GW of coal and 44.9 GW of gas. Those were fuel-input ratings: the sector model turns each plant into a link rated on its fuel side (electric capacity ÷ efficiency). The electric capacities are the official fleet's.已釐清：本頁先前列出燃煤 31.9 GW、燃氣 44.9 GW，那是燃料輸入端的額定值：部門模型把每座電廠轉為以燃料端計量的連結（電力容量 ÷ 效率）。電力容量與官方機組一致。

    What was changed to make it run為了執行所做的修改

    1. The UN energy statistics report Taiwan as "Other Asia", which PyPSA-Earth dropped, so every Taiwan demand was zero. Mapped to Taiwan (checked: 2019 electricity production 274.2 TWh, nuclear 3,872 MW, pumped hydro 2,602 MW).聯合國能源統計將台灣列為「Other Asia」，PyPSA-Earth 因此將其排除，所有台灣需求皆為零。已對應為台灣（核對：2019 年發電量 274.2 TWh、核能 3,872 MW、抽蓄水力 2,602 MW）。
    2. The UN urban-population file moved to a new ID; the script now looks it up. Its name "China, Taiwan Province of" matched two countries and was dropped; mapped to Taiwan.聯合國都市人口檔案的編號已變更，程式改為自動查詢；其名稱「China, Taiwan Province of」同時對應兩個國家而遭排除，已對應為台灣。
    3. The World Port Index could not be downloaded by Python (certificate error); a local copy is used instead.世界港口索引因憑證錯誤無法由 Python 下載，改用本機副本。

    Next steps before publishing發布前的下一步

    1. Decide whether rooftop solar and biomass CHP may be built, or fix them at today's capacity for the 2025 reference.決定是否允許新建屋頂型太陽光電與生質能汽電共生，或在 2025 年基準情境中固定為現有容量。
    2. Check each demand against the Energy Administration's energy balance and align the scope (national vs Taipower system).逐項與能源署能源平衡表核對需求，並統一範圍（全國或台電系統）。
    3. Decide which technologies are fixed and which the model may build; set a CO₂ path.決定哪些技術固定、哪些可由模型新建；設定 CO₂ 路徑。
    4. Use finer time steps (e.g. 3–4 hours) once the inputs are right.輸入正確後，改用較細的時間步長（例如 3–4 小時）。
