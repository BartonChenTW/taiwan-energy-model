An open model of Taiwan's electricity system台灣電力系統的開放模型

What does it take to keep Taiwan's lights on, and cut its emissions? 要讓台灣穩定供電、同時減少排放，需要什麼？

Taiwan imports 99% of its energy, runs an island grid with no links to its neighbours, and must grow its supply for a booming chip industry while moving away from coal. This site uses an open-source model (PyPSA-Earth) with official Taiwanese data to explore these questions: hour by hour, over a full year. 台灣 99% 的能源仰賴進口，電網是無對外聯網的孤島，還要在減少燃煤的同時，為蓬勃發展的半導體產業增加供電。本網站以開源模型（PyPSA-Earth）與台灣官方資料，逐時模擬一整年，探討這些問題。
