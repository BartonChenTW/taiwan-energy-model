Taiwan's power grid runs as a long north–south 345 kV spine backed by 161 kV and 69 kV loops, while almost all fossil fuel enters through LNG receiving terminals, coal ports, and coastal refineries connected by subsea and onshore trunk pipelines. This page brings together official Taipower and MOEA network data, OpenStreetMap spatial objects, PyPSA-Earth network aggregation, and gas/fuel infrastructure. 台灣電力網以南北向 345 kV 超高壓主幹線為骨幹，搭配 161 kV 與 69 kV 區域迴路；幾乎所有化石燃料則經由液化天然氣（LNG）接收站、燃煤卸煤港及沿海煉油廠輸入，並透過海陸輸氣幹線串聯。本頁彙整經濟部與台電官方電網統計、OpenStreetMap（OSM）空間圖資、PyPSA-Earth 路網簡化過程與天然氣／燃料供應網路。
