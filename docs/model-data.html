What the model is built from, in full detail: the grid and its six regions, every power plant, where renewables can go and how much they could produce, and what each technology costs. These are the inputs of today's system (2013 weather) and of the MOEA 2030 and 2034 plans; results are on the model dashboard.模型的完整輸入資料：電網與六個區域、每座電廠、再生能源可設置的位置與可發電量，以及各技術的成本。資料涵蓋現況系統（2013 年氣象）與經濟部 2030、2034 年規劃；模擬結果請見模型儀表板。

This fork runs PyPSA-Earth, an open model with its own global data. Some inputs are PyPSA-Earth's defaults, some are Taiwan data this fork adds, and some are other sources or settings added here. PyPSA's cost data compared with Taiwan'sPyPSA 成本資料與台灣資料比較 PyPSA-Earth defaultPyPSA-Earth 預設 Taiwan data台灣資料

The model uses PyPSA's technology-data, mostly European (Danish Energy Agency) figures. Taiwan publishes its own: the Ministry of Economic Affairs sets feed-in tariffs from an installed cost, an O&M share, an annual output and a cost of capital for each technology, and Taipower reports the actual cost of each source. Below, the Taiwan figures sit next to the PyPSA rows of cost year .模型使用 PyPSA 的 technology-data，多為歐洲（丹麥能源署）數據。台灣有自己的資料：經濟部依各技術的期初設置成本、運維比例、年售電量與平均資金成本率訂定躉購費率；台電公布各種發電方式的實際成本。以下將台灣數據與 PyPSA 年成本並列比較。
