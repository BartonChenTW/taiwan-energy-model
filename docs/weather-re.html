Hourly ERA5 reanalysis weather (2013 and 2018, 0.3° resolution) drives every renewable profile in the model through Atlite, combined with Copernicus 100 m land cover, WDPA protected areas, GEBCO 2025 ocean bathymetry, and HydroBASINS level-6 catchments. Explore the spatial climate fields, monsoon wind roses, extreme lull periods (Dunkelflaute), and full technical potential across Taiwan and its Exclusive Economic Zone. 本模型以逐時 ERA5 再分析氣象資料（2013 與 2018 年、0.3° 解析度）透過 Atlite 轉換為各類再生能源出力曲線，並結合 Copernicus 100 公尺土地利用、WDPA 保護區、GEBCO 2025 海底水深及 HydroBASINS 第 6 級流域圖資。本頁完整呈現氣象空間分布、季風風花圖、極端低風光期與全台及專屬經濟區（EEZ）技術潛力。
